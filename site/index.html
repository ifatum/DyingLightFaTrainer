01  The menu

Fourteen pages, grouped like the game

The sidebar on the left is the trainer's own. Pages that have something switched on get a dot, and Turn all off puts every cheat and slider back at once.

Player

Your own character, in the story and in co-op.

  • God mode, also against the forced damage Harran Prison uses.
  • Infinite stamina, grappling hook and UV flashlight, or a slider that makes the UV charge last up to ten times longer.
  • Instant lockpicking. Every pick position is the sweet spot.
  • No fall damage, movement speed, jump height, refill health and stamina.

Combat

Weapons and enemies.

  • One hit kill. Be The Zombie nests keep their real health.
  • Infinite ammo, no reload, infinite consumables.
  • Unbreakable weapons: each weapon keeps its own durability full.
  • Kill every zombie and human within 80 m through the game's own kill, so quest counters move.

Skills

Every skill tree, the Night Hunter level and both PvP ranks.

  • XP multiplier, or level up through real XP so the game hands out the skill points.
  • Set any tree: +1, +10, Max, -1, -10.
  • Survivor and Night Hunter ranks for Be The Zombie.

Night Hunter

For Be The Zombie, when you invade as the hunter.

  • Hunter god mode and infinite energy. UV light no longer exhausts you.
  • No ability cooldowns: tendril, camouflage and ground pound are always ready.
  • Infinite spits, and your own key for each of the four spit types.
  • Long camouflage: ten minutes, and you can run and attack while hidden.

PvP

How far each attack reaches. Each player's game decides its own attacks, so some sliders only change what happens on your side.

  • Pounce, ground pound, tackle, claws, death from above, dropkick, kicks and melee.
  • At Max the pounce, dropkick and death from above also take targets that are not in front of you.
  • Spit hit radius: blast spits reach up to 30 m from where they land, toxic puddles grow with it.
  • Death from above height: a normal jump is enough instead of a long drop.

Visuals

See players through walls, and change how the UV light looks.

  • Player ESP: box, role, health bar and number, distance, PvP rank, hunter rage, lines from the bottom of the screen.
  • Allies on or off, a range limit, separate colors for the Night Hunter and survivors.
  • Your own UV light color and glow strength.

Prison

The Harran Prison DLC run.

  • Pause both prison timers.
  • Teleport to the start, every split, the reward room and the evacuation.
  • Record your route once, then replay it so every quest trigger fires.
  • Save and load your position.

Cash to Give items

Cash, Backpack, Stash, Materials, Tools and Give items.

  • Set your money and any stack count.
  • Spawn any weapon, upgrade, consumable, material or blueprint. Each goes into the inventory the game accepts it in.
  • Weapon editor: damage, durability, crits, knockback, stamina use, reach, magazine, reload time, upgrades, repairs and rarity.

Settings

The menu itself.

  • Accent color, interface size, background dim, menu key.
  • Sidebar order and which pages show.
  • Named configs: save the cheats and sliders you use and load them in one click.

02  Install

Copy one file

The game loads xinput1_3.dll from its own folder, so the trainer starts with it. Delete the file to remove it.

Windows

  1. Download the zip and take xinput1_3.dll out of it.

  2. In Steam, right-click Dying Light, then Manage, then Browse local files.

  3. Put the file next to DyingLightGame.exe and start the game.

  4. Press Insert or F8 once you are in game.

Linux, Steam with Proton

  1. Put xinput1_3.dll next to DyingLightGame.exe, as on Windows.

  2. Tell Proton to use it. In the game's Properties, paste this into Launch options:

    WINEDLLOVERRIDES="xinput1_3=n,b" %command%

  3. Start the game and press Insert or F8.

03  Before you play

Read this first

  • Back up your saves

    Item, level and rank changes are saved by the game like any other progress. Keep a copy of your save folder before you try something big.

  • Other players

    In co-op and Be The Zombie the trainer changes your game, and through it, theirs. Turn things off when you play with people who did not agree to it.

  • Techland's rules

    Modifying the game online may break Dying Light's terms of use. Any action Techland takes against an account is between you and Techland.

  • If something breaks

    The trainer writes fatrainer.log next to the game. Attach it when you report a problem on GitHub.
